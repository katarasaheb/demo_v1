The iStack

(The Intelligence Infrastructure of Modern Grocery)

Designed from First Principles

Most grocery platforms optimize on top of an inherited system. Kitchin starts earlier — at the physics of food. The iStack is our proprietary intelligence framework, unifying multiple subsystems to power fresher, more transparent, and scalable grocery delivery.

iBakery

Automated, predictive baking and production ensuring freshness, minimal waste, and optimized supply for all bakery products.

iStudio

Content and media production engine for recipe development, consumer engagement, and educational experiences to increase retention.

iSupply

Local sourcing, hydroponics, and predictive yield management to provide consistent, high-quality inventory with minimal intermediaries.

iPricing

Transparent, dynamic pricing engine that accounts for cost, demand, and market intelligence — eliminating hidden markups.

iLogistics

AI-driven routing, micro-distribution hubs, and demand-led fulfillment for faster, efficient delivery and reduced environmental impact.

iIncentives

Data-driven alignment for producers, retailers, and consumers through loyalty, profit-sharing, and behavior-based rewards.

iAnalytics

Continuous feedback from operations, consumer behavior, and market trends to refine forecasting, supply, and delivery intelligence.

iExperience

Personalized consumer journey through curated recommendations, fresh product insights, and seamless digital interaction.

How the iStack Works

Each layer of the iStack communicates seamlessly to create an end-to-end intelligent grocery ecosystem:

  • iSupply forecasts inventory needs based on predictive analytics from iAnalytics and trends captured by iStudio.
  • iBakery receives demand signals from iSupply to schedule production with minimal waste and peak freshness.
  • iPricing sets real-time, transparent prices based on supply, demand, and consumer behavior insights from iAnalytics.
  • iLogistics optimizes routes and fulfillment timing based on live inventory and order data, ensuring fast delivery.
  • iIncentives aligns producers and consumers, rewarding behaviors that reduce waste and improve efficiency.
  • iExperience delivers a personalized consumer journey, integrating data from iStudio (content) and iAnalytics (behavior tracking).
  • All layers continuously feed insights into iAnalytics, creating a closed-loop system of learning, optimization, and adaptation.

The result is a **self-optimizing grocery ecosystem**: inventory stays fresh, supply meets demand precisely, delivery is efficient, and consumer experience is personalized and engaging.
