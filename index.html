The Canadian Grocery Crisis

Canada’s grocery system faces rising prices, supply chain inefficiencies, and limited consumer choice. These systemic issues create challenges for both households and businesses nationwide.

Price Fixing

75% market control by a few retailers drives higher prices and limits affordability for consumers.

Supply Waste

Long, inefficient supply chains result in significant food waste and operational inefficiencies.

Cost Inflation

Tariffs, import disruptions, and rising operational costs further increase grocery prices.

Obsolescence

Traditional stores are slow to innovate, maintaining outdated models that fail to meet modern consumer needs.
